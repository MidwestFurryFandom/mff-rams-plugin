How to Find Us

Accessible Registration Queue - Convention Center (Hall A)
The Queue will be open Thursday through Saturday during the same hours as Registration.

Once you arrive at our information desk, a staff member will check you in and give you a numbered ticket. Feel free to sit anywhere in the waiting area until your number is called. If you need support -- for example, if you are blind or have low vision and would like a sighted critter to guide you -- just let the check-in staffer know and they'll be happy to help.

Accessibility Services Office - Hyatt Regency O'Hare (Lambert)
Our office is located on the entry level of the Hyatt Regency. Stop by and say hello! Team members are available Friday and Saturday from 10am to 8pm, and Sunday from 10am to 5pm.

Our office is your hub for accessibility questions and real-time support throughout the weekend. If you didn't get a wristband at badge pickup or need a replacement, you can get one here. We also offer helpful items like facemasks, earplugs, hand sanitizer, and a small selection of sensory tools.

Look for the Green Vests!
Accessibility Services staff will be wearing green vests throughout the convention. Our team will be circulating the convention space to identify and address accessibility barriers. If you encounter a barrier or need assistance, please don't hesitate to approach a staff member wearing a green vest.

You can also reach the office by phone during operating hours at: 530-495-4633 Ext. 303.
